  <big><strong>Irreducibility and Complete Reducibility</big></strong> <br>

  The distinction between irreducible and completely reducible representations was reviewed. The representation operators D(g) were interpreted as linear transformations acting on the representation space. An irreducible representation was characterized by the absence of any nontrivial invariant subspace. <br>

  Completely reducible representations were described as representations that can be decomposed into a direct sum of irreducible invariant subspaces. In an appropriately chosen basis, the corresponding representation matrices can be written in block-diagonal form. <br><br>
